# Example project onboarding

This is synthetic demonstration content. Replace it with your own documents.

## First day

New team members should first read the project handbook and then arrange an
introductory meeting with their team lead. Access to production systems requires
an explicit approval from the service owner; completing onboarding does not
automatically grant production access.

## Weekly notes

The team stores its weekly onboarding notes in the shared project documentation
folder under `onboarding/weekly-notes`. Notes should record unresolved questions
and the person responsible for the next action.

## Reviews

The team reviews onboarding progress every Friday. A new team member can ask
their team lead for an earlier review when an access issue blocks their work.

## Retention

This example handbook does not specify a retention period for onboarding notes.
